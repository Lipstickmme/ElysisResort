Sunset Sailing

The best three hours available on this island, and the reason to keep one evening free.

Elysis keeps a twelve metre catamaran on a mooring in the bay. She takes ten guests comfortably, with a skipper and one crew, and there is a tender to get you off the beach without wading.

The standard run leaves at six, crosses to the Antiparos side, anchors for a swim in a cove that has no road to it, and comes back with the light going. Mezze and a cold box of wine are aboard. Longer day charters to Despotiko and the small islands can be arranged with a day's notice.

What is included

  • Skipper and crew
  • Mezze and wine aboard
  • Swim stop and snorkelling gear
  • Tender from the beach
