Yoga and Movement

Held at seven, when the sea is flat and the deck is still cool underfoot.

Practice is at seven, Tuesday to Sunday, on the deck built out over the rock at the north end of the property. It is unhurried hatha most mornings, with a longer vinyasa on Saturdays, and everyone from complete beginners upwards comes.

Private sessions, pilates and breathwork can be booked in the spa or on your own terrace. Two of our instructors also run a swim and stretch morning, which is exactly what it sounds like.

What is included

  • Mats and props
  • All levels welcome
  • Private sessions on request
  • Breathwork and pilates
