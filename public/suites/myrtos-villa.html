Myrtos sits at the top of the grove where the trees are thickest. Both bedrooms are the same size and have the same bathroom, so nobody is quietly given the worse room.

The terrace is shaded by the grove itself for most of the day, which keeps the house ten degrees cooler than the sea-facing villas in August and makes long lunches at the outdoor table possible at any hour.
