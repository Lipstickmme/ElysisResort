Anemos Residence

The first of the residences: a house with staff attached. Breakfast is cooked in your own kitchen, and dinner can be too.

Anemos takes the whole west headland. The living room opens on two sides, the pool runs twelve metres along the seaward edge, and the kitchen is a working one, with a six burner range and a cold room rather than a token hob.

A residence booking includes a cook for breakfast each morning and the option of a chef for dinner, arranged the day before. Guests who take Anemos for a week usually eat in three or four nights.
