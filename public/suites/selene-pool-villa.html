Selene was built as the pair to Helios and set on the opposite slope. The change of aspect matters more than the plan: the terrace here is shaded by two o'clock and cool by five, which in July is worth more than another hour of sun.

A copper bath stands on the upper terrace under a reed canopy, filled on request with hot water and sea salt. It is the detail guests write to us about afterwards.
