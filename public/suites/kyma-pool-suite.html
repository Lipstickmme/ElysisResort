Kyma Pool Suite

Built on the point, where the terrace wall is the last thing between you and open water. The pool is cut to catch the sun until the moment it goes.

Kyma stands alone on the western point, which gives it two things nothing else here has: no neighbour on either side, and a straight, uninterrupted sunset from the water. The pool edge is set flush with the terrace so that swimming in it, the sea and the pool read as one surface.

The room behind is deliberately simple. A vaulted ceiling, a wall of cedar joinery, and a bed you can see the sea from without sitting up.
