Aithra occupies the entire upper floor of the main house. The interior is two bedroom suites and a living room; the point of it is what is outside, where the terrace runs the full footprint of the building with a pool along the western edge and an outdoor kitchen at the other end.

The bar sends someone up at seven each evening unless you tell them not to. It is the residence taken for anniversaries and, twice a season, for a proposal.
