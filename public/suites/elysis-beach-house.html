At the far end of the private beach, where the rock closes the bay. Step off the deck onto sand, and there is nobody between you and the water.

The Beach House was the original fisherman's store and is the only structure at sea level. It was rebuilt in timber and stone with a deck across the whole front, a pool set into it, and two bedroom suites behind.

Its own stretch of beach is screened by the rock at the south end, so it stays private even when the beach club is busy. The bonfire on Thursday nights is lit fifty metres from the deck, which guests either love or ask us to move; it does not move.
