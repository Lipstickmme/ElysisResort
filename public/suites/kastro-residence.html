Kastro Residence

The oldest structure on the estate and the largest of the three bedroom residences. The tower is now the library; everything else was built around it.

The watchtower on this corner was a ruin when the land was bought, and the condition of keeping it was written into the plans. Its ground floor is now a small library with the original stone left exposed, and the residence wraps around it in two low wings.

Three bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, a living room that seats ten, and a fourteen metre pool along the terrace edge. At night the lights of Naoussa are visible across the water from the pool.
