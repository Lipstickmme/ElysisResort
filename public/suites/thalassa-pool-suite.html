Thalassa Pool Suite

The first of the pool suites, and the one closest to the water. The pool is heated from April and screened on three sides, so it is yours from breakfast until well after dark.

Thalassa takes the lower terrace, where the ground drops towards the sea path and the walls can be kept low without losing privacy. The pool is four metres by three, heated in the shoulder months, and lit from below at night.

Inside, the room is arranged around the view rather than the bed: a long window seat under the west window, and the bed set back where it stays dark in the morning.
