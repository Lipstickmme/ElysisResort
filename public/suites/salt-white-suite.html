The Salt White rooms sit around a small courtyard on the landward side, where the walls hold the cool and the only sound after eleven is the wind in the fig. They are the rooms our own team book when family visits.

Everything inside is identical to the sea-facing suites. What you trade is the horizon, and what you get back is silence and a rate that lets you stay a week instead of three nights.
