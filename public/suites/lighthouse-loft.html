The lower floor holds the two bedrooms, cut into the rock and cool all day. Above them the living room opens on three sides, and above that the roof terrace looks over the whole bay with nothing in the way.

Sunrise and sunset are both visible from the roof, which is true of no other residence here. Breakfast is served up there if you want it, and the bar will set up for eight.
