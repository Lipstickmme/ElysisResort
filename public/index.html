01 — The house

Eighteen residences above a private bay.

Elysis sits on the western shoulder of Kolymbithres Bay, where the granite runs into the sea and the wind drops after five. The resort is built the way the island builds: lime-washed walls a metre thick, cedar shutters, stone underfoot, nothing taller than the olive trees it was set among.

There are eighteen residences and no more. That number is the whole idea. It means a terrace nobody overlooks, a beach that never fills, a kitchen that can cook for you rather than for a service, and a team that learns your name on the first morning rather than the last.
