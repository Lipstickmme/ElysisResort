Getting here

Paros is easier to reach than it looks.

By air

Paros National Airport (PAS), 22 minutes by car. Athens (ATH) connects four times daily in season.

By sea

Parikia port, 18 minutes by car. Fast ferries from Piraeus in 3h 15m, Mykonos in 45m.

Transfers

Private car, launch, or helicopter arranged by the concierge before you travel.

The house

Arrival from 15:00, departure by 12:00. Both move when the house allows it.
